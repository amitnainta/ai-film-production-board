# TOCK — script

```
TOCK
A 5-minute 3D animated short. Nearly wordless.

1. EXT. CITY – NIGHT
A hillside city glows orange. Above it, not a single star.

INT. ARUN'S BEDROOM – NIGHT
ARUN (9) lies awake under a blue night light. He holds a framed photo: GRANDPA, smiling, pointing up at a sky full of stars. Arun looks to the window. Orange haze. Nothing.
His eyes narrow. He looks up.

INT. HALLWAY – NIGHT
Arun tugs the attic cord. The ladder drops in a puff of dust.

2. INT. ATTIC – NIGHT
Moonlight through a round window. Flashlight sweeping trunks. Arun finds a long leather case, a tag in Grandpa's handwriting. Latches click. Inside: a brass telescope on red velvet, and beside it a tiny brass firefly, dark, one wing bent. Arun nudges it. Nothing.
He carries it all to GRANDPA'S WORKBENCH and switches on the lamp.

3. INT. ATTIC – WORKBENCH – CONTINUOUS
Tweezers. Magnifying glass. A drop of oil. Arun straightens the wing, oils the gear, turns the tiny key.
The belly-light flickers amber, then glows. TOCK lifts its head and looks at him.
Tock's first flight: a wobble, a bonk off the lampshade, a tumble into Arun's hair.
Arun laughs for the first time. Tock peeks out and blinks.

4. EXT. ROOFTOP – NIGHT
Arun sets up the telescope by the water tank and props Grandpa's photo on the ledge, just like in the picture. He aims up and looks.
Through the eyepiece: orange haze.
He slides down against the tank and hugs his knees. Tock hovers in front of him. Its light dims.

5. EXT. CITY – NIGHT
Tock's light flares. It launches into the night, a single amber spark across the rooftops.
Across the street, a GIRL in a yellow hoodie sees the blinking light and switches off her lamp to see it better. Then her neighbour. Then the next window. Tock hovers over the street, blinking.
Block by block, the city goes dark. Down a long avenue, streetlights switch off one by one.
On the roof, Arun stands. The orange fades from his face.

6. EXT. ROOFTOP – NIGHT
The Milky Way pours across the sky.
Arun runs to the telescope. Stars everywhere, and the constellation Grandpa is pointing at in the photo.
He steps back and just looks up.
Tock returns, lands on the telescope and folds its wings, light flickering. Arun cups his hands around it.

7. EXT. ROOFTOP – NIGHT
Arun leans the photo against the telescope.
ARUN (whispering)
Goodnight, Grandpa.
Tock's light blinks twice. High above, one star blinks back.

TITLE: TOCK
```
